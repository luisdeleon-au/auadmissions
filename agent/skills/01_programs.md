# Skill: IT Programs & Curriculum
## Atlantis University — Department of Information Technology

---

## Degree Structure Overview

Atlantis University offers two undergraduate IT degrees:

| Degree | Knowledge Areas | AU Certificates | Industry Certs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Associate of Science in IT | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Bachelor of Science in IT | 5 (includes all 3 Associate areas) | 5 | 6 |

**Key framing for reps:** These are not two separate programs — they are one road with two exit ramps. The Associate is complete and job-ready on its own. The Bachelor builds directly on top of it. Every Associate credit transfers.

---

## Applied Generative AI — Universal Integration

**Every knowledge area in both degrees includes Applied Generative AI.** This is not an elective or a separate course — it is woven into every module across all five knowledge areas.

Students learn three AI skill categories:
- **Prompt Engineering** — writing instructions that get reliable, professional-quality results from AI models
- **Context Engineering** — structuring the full context of a problem so AI tools understand the situation, not just the question
- **LLM Tool Mastery** — hands-on practice with ChatGPT, Claude AI, Claude Code, Gemini, Meta AI, and Grok

**Admissions talking point:** "Our students don't just learn IT — they learn to amplify their IT work with AI. Every course includes hands-on AI skills. Graduates are AI-ready, not just IT-ready."

## Associate of Science in IT

### Knowledge Area 1: Programming & Operating Systems

**Plain-English description:** How computers think — and how to make them do what you want. Covers software fundamentals and operating system management.

**Who it's for:** Logical thinkers, problem solvers, students who want to understand how software works or automate tasks.

**Industry certification:** Python Institute PCEP (Entry-Level Python Programmer)
**AU internal certificate:** Certified MIS Associate

**One-sentence pitch:** "If you like thinking through problems step by step and want to work with software, this is your starting point — and you'll earn a Python certification on the way."

#### Courses

| Code | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| CIT 102 | Introduction to Programming | First steps in Python — logic, variables, loops, problem-solving. Zero experience needed. |
| CIT 105 | Programming Practice | Applied Python: functions, file handling, real mini-projects. |
| CIT 110 | Computer Operating Systems | Windows and Linux internals — how an OS manages hardware and software. |
| CIT 225 | Management Information Systems | How businesses use IT systems to make decisions — the bridge between tech and business. |

---

### Knowledge Area 2: Network Technology

**Plain-English description:** The backbone of every company — designing, running, and fixing the networks that connect every device in an organization.

**Who it's for:** Hands-on fixers, students who want to work in corporate IT infrastructure, people who like to understand how systems connect and communicate.

**Industry certification:** CompTIA Network+
**AU internal certificate:** Certified Network Operations Associate

**One-sentence pitch:** "If you're the kind of person who wants to understand how everything connects — and be the one who keeps it running — Network Technology is for you. You'll work with Cisco equipment and earn your CompTIA Network+ along the way."

**Note:** AU is a CompTIA Academic Partner. Certification prep is built into the curriculum, not bolted on as test prep.

#### Courses

| Code | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| CIT 280 | Network Design | Topologies, protocols, and how to architect a corporate network from scratch. |
| CIT 281 | Network Administration & Support | Day-to-day operations: Cisco configs, troubleshooting, help desk scenarios. |
| CIT 282 | Advanced Network Administration | Advanced routing, switching, VLANs, enterprise-level configurations. |
| CIT 283 | Networking: Azure and AWS | Cloud networking — virtual networks, firewalls, hybrid infrastructures. |

## Bachelor of Science in IT

**Plain-English description:** Every application runs on data — this area teaches students to design, query, and manage the databases that power every modern system.

### Knowledge Area 5: Cybersecurity

**Plain-English description:** The most in-demand IT specialty — protecting organizations from threats, managing risk, and ensuring systems stay secure.

**Who it's for:** Protectors, investigators, defenders. Students drawn to puzzles, security challenges, and the idea of staying ahead of attackers.

**Industry certifications:** CompTIA Security+ AND CompTIA CySA+
**AU internal certificate:** Certified Cybersecurity Associate

**One-sentence pitch:** "Cybersecurity is the most in-demand specialty in IT right now. You'll learn to protect systems, analyze threats, and even ethically hack — earning both CompTIA Security+ and CySA+ along the way."

**Common misconception to address:** Most cybersecurity jobs are defenders, not hackers. Security Analysts, IT Auditors, Compliance Officers, Risk Managers. CIT 481 (Ethical Hacking) exists so defenders understand how attackers think — not to produce hackers.

#### Courses

| Code | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| CIT 480 | Security Technology | Firewalls, IDS/IPS, encryption — the professional security toolkit. |
| CIT 482 | Security Analyst | Monitoring systems, reading logs, detecting threats — the defender's mindset. |
| CIT 481 | Security Ethical Hacking | Thinking like an attacker to find vulnerabilities first. |
| CIT 483 | Information Systems Security | Policies, compliance, risk management — the business side of security. |
| CIT 484 | Advanced Information Systems Security | Threat intelligence, incident response, security architecture. |

## Prerequisites

**None.** Every knowledge area starts at the foundational level:
- CIT 102 assumes zero programming experience
- CIT 280 assumes zero networking experience
- CIT 381 assumes zero Linux experience
- CIT 406 assumes zero database experience
- CIT 480 assumes zero security experience

The program is explicitly designed for career changers and first-time learners, not just students with prior IT backgrounds.

---

## The Associate → Bachelor Pathway

- The Associate is designed as the first phase of the Bachelor's
- Every Associate credit transfers — no credit loss, no re-application
- Students who complete the Associate and choose to continue simply add Database Systems and Cybersecurity
- They keep all certificates earned during the Associate
- **Rep framing for cost-conscious prospects:** "You can start with the Associate, get working in IT, and continue to the Bachelor's later. Nothing is lost."
