## Associate of Science in Information Technology
**60 Credit Hours — Estimated Completion Time: 20 months**

### Program Description (Official)
The Information Technology program focuses on computing, computer science, and information science and systems. Designed to prepare students for employment in various industries that utilize computer skills and knowledge. Students gain knowledge in: programming, database management, networking, enterprise cloud technology, and security. Courses include theory and skill building. Students have access to the computer lab to complete projects.

### Official Program Objectives
1. Equip students with knowledge to evaluate IT infrastructure needs for an organization
2. Empower students to design, implement, and evaluate a computer-based system, process, component, or program to meet industry needs
3. Prepare students with technical knowledge and critical-thinking skills for an IT career
4. Apply technological skills in hardware, networking, security, cloud computing, database, web development, IT project management, and research to critically analyze and solve problems

## Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
**123 Credit Hours — Estimated Completion Time: 41 months**

### Program Description (Official)
Provides students with a solid foundation in business and technology, developing skills to effectively apply information technology to improve business processes across a broad spectrum of disciplines. Students demonstrate advanced skills in business application software, current programming languages, and recognize various phases of the software development cycle.

### Official Program Objectives
Upon completion, students may seek employment in business, government, or a variety of industries where a combination of general business and IT skills are needed. Students will be able to:
- Use information technology to revitalize business and achieve strategic goals
- Identify basic elements of business opportunities in electronic commerce

### Transfer Pathway
Students who have completed an IT Associate's Degree (60 credit hours) or equivalent can transfer those credits and complete the remaining 63 credit hours of major courses for a total of 123 semester credit hours.

## Credit Hour Definitions

- **Semester credit hour:** Each course = 3 credit hours = 48 hours of instruction
- **Semester:** 16-week period of instruction
- **Undergraduate full-time:** Enrolled for minimum 12 semester credit hours
- **Undergraduate part-time:** Enrolled in fewer than 12 semester credit hours
