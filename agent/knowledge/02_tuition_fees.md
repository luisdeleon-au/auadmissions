# Knowledge: Tuition, Fees & Program Costs
## Source: Atlantis University 2026 Catalog, Volume I (Pages 142–144)

---

## Undergraduate Degree Programs (AS & BS)

### Tuition
**$450.00 per credit hour**

### Semester Fee
**$80.00 per credit hour per semester**
- Example: 12 credit hours enrolled → $960.00 semester fee ($80 × 12)

### Application Fee
**$150.00** (one-time, non-refundable)

### Registration Fee
**$50.00 per semester** (every semester, all programs)

### Late Registration Fee
**$100.00** (if registering after established deadline)

### Graduation Fee
**$500.00** (one-time)

### Books and Learning Materials
- Approximately **$100 per course**, additional and separate from tuition
- Students purchase from local bookstores or online providers
- Books are returnable (in "as new" condition) within 20 days of withdrawal

## Important Financial Policies

### Tuition Lock
- Tuition is set by the Enrollment Agreement
- Will NOT change as long as student maintains **consecutive enrollment**
- Any break in enrollment or program change = new enrollment agreement = subject to current rates

### Refund Policy Key Points
- Cancellation within 3 business days of signing → full refund
- Before first class starts → full refund minus registration fee
- After first week of classes (Degree Programs) → **no refund**, student responsible for full semester cost
- Books returnable within 20 days of withdrawal if in "as new" condition

### Financial Aid
- AU is approved for **Title IV federal financial aid** for qualifying students
- Route all financial aid questions to the Financial Aid office

## Rep Guidance on Costs

**When a prospect asks about cost:**
- Give the per-credit-hour figures: "$450 per credit hour for undergrad"
- Mention the Associate (60 credits) vs. Bachelor's (123 credits) difference
- Always recommend they speak with Financial Aid for exact totals and aid options
- Emphasize the tuition lock: "As long as you stay enrolled consecutively, your tuition rate doesn't change"

**When a prospect says "I can't afford it":**
"Let's get you connected with our Financial Aid office — AU is approved for federal financial aid for students who qualify. And one thing worth knowing: the tuition rate is locked in when you enroll, so starting sooner actually protects you from future rate changes. Would it help to talk through what the Associate path looks like from a cost perspective first?"
