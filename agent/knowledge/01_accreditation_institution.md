## Accreditation

**Atlantis University is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC).**

- Initially granted institutional accreditation in 2012
- Renewed in 2016 for an additional 6 years
- Awarded the **2017 School of Excellence** award by ACCSC
- Licensed by the **Commission for Independent Education (CIE), Florida Department of Education**
- License # 3355
- Recognized by the **US Department of Education**
- Approved to award **Title IV funds** for those who qualify (federal financial aid)

## Admissions Process

- Rolling admissions policy (no fixed application deadlines)
- Local applicants encouraged to visit in person
- International and Distance Education applicants may apply online at www.atlantisuniversity.edu
- Students notified of acceptance/rejection after application is submitted
- Equal opportunity/equal access basis for all admissions services

**Rep script for "Is AU accredited?":**
"Yes — Atlantis University is accredited by ACCSC, the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges, and licensed by Florida's Commission for Independent Education. The university is also recognized by the US Department of Education and approved for Title IV financial aid. I can get you the official documentation if you need it."
