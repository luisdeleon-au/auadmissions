# Knowledge: IT Diploma Programs
## Source: Atlantis University 2026 Catalog, Volume I (Pages 106–109)

---

## Overview

Atlantis University offers four IT-related Diploma Programs. These are short, intensive programs that:
- Can be completed faster than degree programs
- Prepare students for specific industry certifications
- **Transfer 100% into the BS Information Technology and BS Computer Engineering degree programs**
- Are offered through the School of Computer Sciences and Technology

---

## 1. Network Operations Diploma (NOP)

**Duration:** 192 Clock Hours / 12 Credit Hours
**Estimated Completion Time:** 4 months
**Tuition:** $8,840 (fixed, all-in)
**Application Fee:** $50

### Program Description
Designed to prepare students to operate, install, diagnose, configure, troubleshoot, upgrade, and maintain microcomputers, while gaining understanding of computer networks. Introduces and prepares students for careers in networking, network operations, network analysis, and network engineering.

### Program Objective
Graduates acquire knowledge, skills, and preparedness to take **CompTIA Network+ and Cisco Certification Exams**.

### Courses
| Course | Title | Clock Hours |
|---|---|---|
| CIT 280 | Network Design (Prep for Network+) | 48 |
| CIT 281 | Network Administration & Technical Support (Prep for Cisco CCNA) | 48 |
| CIT 282 | Advanced Network Administration (Prep for Cisco CCNA) | 48 |
| CIT 283 | Networking on Azure and AWS | 48 |

## 2. Enterprise Cloud Professional Diploma (ECP)

**Duration:** 288 Clock Hours / 18 Credit Hours
**Estimated Completion Time:** 6 months
**Tuition:** $13,260 (fixed, all-in)
**Application Fee:** $50

### Program Description
Focused on cloud standards from Microsoft, NIST, Amazon Web Services, Google, IBM, and others. Designed for students to analyze IT business requirements, design, and implement infrastructure for business solutions based on Microsoft and other platforms.

### Program Objective
Students gain skills to design, configure, and maintain cloud infrastructure — public, private, and hybrid clouds. After completion, students are equipped to take industry certifications including **Microsoft Office 365 Solutions Associate, CompTIA Network+/Linux+, Amazon AWS Associate/Professional**.

### Courses
| Course | Title | Clock Hours |
|---|---|---|
| CIT 280 | Network Design (Prep for Network+) | 48 |
| CIT 381 | Linux Technology (Prep for Linux+) | 48 |
| CIT 382 | Cloud Technology Developer (Prep for AWS Solutions Associate) | 48 |
| CIT 383 | Advanced Cloud Technology Architect | 48 |
| CIT 384 | Manage Cloud Technology Identities & Requirements | 48 |
| CIT 385 | Cloud Technology Compute Services (Prep for AWS Professional) | 48 |

## 3. InfoSec Professional Diploma (ISP)

**Duration:** 240 Clock Hours / 15 Credit Hours
**Estimated Completion Time:** 4 months
**Tuition:** $13,260 (fixed, all-in)
**Application Fee:** $50

### Program Description
Hands-on approach using interactive tools and easy-to-follow labs. Allows quick application of learned concepts. Highly interactive activities that stimulate learning and improve knowledge retention. **No previous technical skills or knowledge required** — only basic PC usage skills.

### Program Objective
Provides foundational networking and security technology knowledge, practical experience, career exploration, and soft-skills development for entry-level IT careers.

**Entry-level roles targeted:** Network installer, network security specialist, security technician, help desk technician, pre-sales support technician, network technician.

### Courses
| Course | Title | Clock Hours | Certification Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| CIT 480 | Security Technology | 48 | CompTIA Security+ |
| CIT 481 | Security Ethical Hacking | 48 | EC Council CEH |
| CIT 482 | Security Analyst | 48 | EC Council ECSA |
| CIT 483 | Information Systems Security | 48 | ISC2 CISSP (foundational) |
| CIT 484 | Advanced Information Systems Security | 48 | ISC2 CISSP |

## 4. Computer Information Technology Diploma (CIT)

**Duration:** 672 Clock Hours / 42 Credit Hours
**Estimated Completion Time:** 14 months
**Tuition:** $28,000 (fixed, all-in)
**Application Fee:** $50

### Program Description
Combines training from Network Operations (NOP), Enterprise Cloud Professional (ECP), and InfoSec Professional (ISP) into one complete IT Professional Program. Broadest knowledge base of all diploma options. Prepares graduates for employment in computer networking with broad IT knowledge.

**Career paths:** Network administrator, data communication manager, communication specialist.

### Program Objective
Prepares students to become IT Professionals using technologies from **CompTIA, Cisco, and Microsoft**. Students gain skills to analyze business requirements, design and implement solutions for clients.

### Curriculum
- 12 credit hours from NOP curriculum (CIT 280, 281, 282, 283)
- At least 15 credit hours from ECP curriculum (CIT 381, 382, 383, 384, 385)
- 15 credit hours from ISP curriculum (CIT 480, 481, 482, 483, 484)

## Key Diploma Program Facts for Reps

### The Transfer Advantage
**100% of diploma program credits transfer into the BS in Information Technology.** This makes diplomas a powerful on-ramp:
- A prospect who isn't ready for a degree program can start with a 4-month NOP diploma ($8,840)
- Get certified and working in IT
- Then apply diploma credits toward the full BS degree

**Rep talking point:** "The NOP diploma is a 4-month program that gets you certified and working — and every credit transfers into the Bachelor's if you decide to continue. It's the fastest on-ramp we have."

### Diploma vs. Degree — When to Recommend Which

| Situation | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Wants to work ASAP, limited time/resources | NOP or ISP Diploma (4 months) |
| Wants cloud focus, 6 months available | ECP Diploma |
| Wants full IT foundation, 14 months | CIT Diploma |
| Wants a full degree credential | AS or BS in IT |
| Unsure, exploring options | Associate → then can continue to Bachelor's |
