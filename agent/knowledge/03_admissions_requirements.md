# Knowledge: Admissions Requirements
## Source: Atlantis University 2026 Catalog, Volume I (Pages 13–20)

---

## General Admissions Requirements (All Degree Programs)

1. Must be **18 years or older** (or have written permission from parent/legal guardian)
2. Complete a recommended **interview with Admissions** (in-person, telephone, or video conference)
3. Complete an **enrollment agreement**
4. Provide proof of **high school completion**:
   - High school diploma, OR
   - High school transcript, OR
   - GED, OR
   - Equivalent document if secondary education was completed in another country
   - **Exception:** Not required if applicant provides official transcripts showing 60+ college credits or an earned degree from an accredited US institution

5. **Documents from non-English speaking countries** must be:
   - Translated into English
   - Evaluated by an approved educational evaluation service

6. **Application fee:** $150.00 (non-refundable) for both undergraduate and graduate programs

7. **Distance Education students** must complete an **online proficiency assessment** evaluating computer literacy, self-discipline, and motivation — must be completed with a passing score prior to enrollment

---

## Undergraduate (AS/BS) Specific Requirements

No additional requirements beyond General Admissions Requirements for most undergraduate programs.

**Transfer students:** Must provide official transcripts from all approved postsecondary schools if seeking transfer credit.
- Acceptable institutions: accredited by US Department of Education-recognized agencies, OR recognized by the Department/Ministry of Education of the country where the institution operates

## Key Admission Facts for Reps

### Prerequisites for IT Programs
**There are NO technical prerequisites.** Students do not need prior IT knowledge, programming experience, or technical background to be admitted. The programs are designed for beginners.

### Transcript Flexibility
For students from Cuba, Venezuela, Puerto Rico, Russia — where official transcripts are difficult or impossible to obtain — the school may accept a copy with a student attestation. Documents must still be translated to English.

### Rolling Admissions
AU uses a rolling admissions policy — there is no single application deadline. Students apply and are notified of acceptance after submission.

### Transfer Credit
Students who have completed an Information Technology Associate's Degree (60 credit hours) or equivalent can transfer those credits and complete only the remaining 63 credit hours of major courses to earn the Bachelor's.

## What the Admissions Process Looks Like

1. Contact Admissions office or apply online at www.atlantisuniversity.edu
2. Complete application (local: encouraged to visit in person; distance/international: apply online)
3. Submit required documents (HS diploma/transcripts, etc.)
4. Recommended interview with Admissions staff
5. Receive acceptance/rejection notification
6. Complete enrollment agreement and pay application fee

## Rep Scripts for Admissions Questions

**"Do I need experience to apply?"**
"No — there are no technical prerequisites for any of our IT programs. CIT 102, our first programming course, assumes zero experience. Same with networking, cloud, and security. The only requirement is a high school diploma or equivalent."

**"I'm from another country — can I still apply?"**
"Yes. International students are welcome to apply online. If your documents are in another language, they'll need to be translated and evaluated by an approved service — the Admissions office can guide you through exactly what's needed."

**"I graduated from a community college — does that count toward the degree?"**
"Possibly yes. If you have 60 college credits from an accredited institution, those could transfer toward our Bachelor's degree. The Registrar would evaluate your specific transcripts — that's something Admissions can arrange for you."

**"How long does it take to get accepted?"**
"We use rolling admissions, so there's no specific deadline — you apply and we notify you after reviewing your application. The office is open Monday through Friday, 9am to 8pm, and Saturday by appointment."
